# Yesteryear: the short watch-through

Third Moon Interactive's own telling of the story. It has the same plot as the episode, but every scene and line here is new, written for our game. It's a non-interactive first pass to watch from start to finish. Choices and puzzles come later, in the gaps marked **[PLAY LATER]**.

Running time: about 6 to 8 minutes. Widescreen, subtitles at the bottom, as in Vulcan Fury.

---

## 1. The Guardian's planet: dusk

*Wind across broken columns. The Guardian's ring glows, and the mist inside it slows and settles. A HISTORIAN stands at a portable recorder. DR McCOY sits on a fallen block, bored.*

**HISTORIAN:** They're coming through. Recording off.

*KIRK steps out of the light, then SPOCK.*

**KIRK:** Bones, you should have come. A whole civilisation lighting its first fires, and we couldn't so much as kick a pebble.

**McCOY:** *(standing, looking past him)* Jim. Who's your friend?

**KIRK:** Very funny.

**McCOY:** I'm not joking. I've never seen that Vulcan in my life.

*Spock looks at McCoy, then at the Guardian. He says nothing. The wind picks up.*

**KIRK:** *(flipping open his communicator)* Kirk to Enterprise. Four to beam up. *(a beat)* Make that five.

---

## 2. Enterprise: transporter room

*They shimmer in. SCOTTY is at the transporter console. An ANDORIAN in a science-blue tunic stands on the deck to meet them: COMMANDER THELIN, the first officer.*

**SCOTTY:** Welcome back, Captain. I had a historian down for that pad. I didnae have a Vulcan.

**THELIN:** Captain. Welcome back. *(to Spock, politely and coolly)* We weren't told to expect a guest.

**KIRK:** Guest? Mister Spock is my first officer.

**THELIN:** With respect, sir, I've held that post for five years. I would have noticed.

**McCOY:** *(from the pad)* He has, Jim. Commander Thelin. You've signed every one of his fitness reports.

*Kirk looks from one to the other. Spock steps down from the pad.*

**SPOCK:** Captain. I believe we should find out what has changed before we decide who is mistaken.

---

## 3. Enterprise: briefing room

*Kirk, Spock, McCoy and Thelin around the table. The wall screen shows a Starfleet records page.*

**THELIN:** The computer has no Spock in Starfleet, past or present.

**KIRK:** Then check Vulcan. His father is Ambassador Sarek.

*The screen changes: SAREK, older and alone in an official portrait.*

**THELIN:** *(reading)* Sarek of Vulcan. Ambassador. One son. *(he pauses)* The boy died young, during his first ordeal in the desert. The mother left Vulcan soon after. She did not reach Earth.

*Silence. Spock's hands are still on the table, very still.*

**McCOY:** *(quietly)* Spock. I'm sorry. Whoever you are, I'm sorry.

**SPOCK:** I did not die in that desert, Doctor. Someone came for me. A cousin. I barely remember him. He called himself Selek. *(a beat)* I believe I now know why I never saw him again.

**KIRK:** It was you.

**SPOCK:** It must have been. And today, while the Guardian was showing the historians Vulcan's past, I was somewhere else entirely.

---

## 4. The Guardian's planet: night

*The ring glows brighter in the dark. Spock now wears a hooded Vulcan travelling robe and carries a simple satchel. Kirk stands with him. Thelin waits a little apart.*

**KIRK:** *(to the Guardian)* Can he put it right?

**GUARDIAN:** *(a vast voice from the stone)* The way is open. What was, may be again, if the traveller changes nothing more.

**THELIN:** *(stepping forward)* Commander. If you succeed, I'm the one who was never here.

**SPOCK:** That is likely.

**THELIN:** Then go well. I'd rather never have had this chair than keep it at the cost of a dead child.

*Spock inclines his head to him.* **[PLAY LATER: a choice of how Spock answers Thelin.]**

**SPOCK:** Captain. If I do not return...

**KIRK:** You'll return. That's an order.

*Spock steps into the light and is gone.*

---

## 5. Shi'Kahr, thirty years ago: a city street at sunset

*Red sky, the great domes of the city, a market closing for the evening. SPOCK (as Selek, hood down) walks slowly, taking it in.*

**SPOCK:** *(voice-over, log)* Personal log. Shi'Kahr, thirty years before my own time. The city is louder than I remember it. Or I was smaller.

*Off to one side, three VULCAN BOYS have cornered a smaller boy, YOUNG SPOCK.*

**VULCAN BOY:** Go on. Show us your Vulcan control, half-blood.

*Young Spock shoves him. The boys scatter, pleased. They got the reaction they wanted.*

*SAREK arrives, too late to see the start of it, in time to see the shove. Young Spock looks at the ground.*

**SAREK:** Go home, Spock.

*The boy goes. Sarek notices the stranger watching.*

**SAREK:** You have seen my family at its worst, traveller.

**SPOCK:** I have seen a boy who was provoked, Ambassador. I am Selek, a distant cousin on your mother's side, travelling to the old shrine in the hills.

**SAREK:** *(studying his face a moment too long)* Then you will not travel tonight. My house is close.

**[PLAY LATER: Selek earns Sarek's trust; wrong answers ripple the timeline.]**

---

## 6. Sarek's house: the courtyard, evening

*A walled courtyard of warm stone. An enormous, shaggy, sleepy SEHLAT, I-CHAYA, lies by the fountain. Young Spock sits against its side. AMANDA brings Selek a cup.*

**AMANDA:** He'll be in that spot until someone carries him to bed. The sehlat, I mean, though it's true of my son as well.

**SPOCK:** They seem well suited.

**AMANDA:** I-Chaya was Sarek's when he was a boy. He's old now. *(she watches her son)* Spock goes into the desert for his test next month. His father says he's ready. Spock doesn't believe him.

*Spock looks at the boy for a long moment.*

**SPOCK:** *(voice-over)* He is not going next month. He is going tonight. I remember the plan now, though I did not think it a plan at the time.

**[PLAY LATER: Selek's night in the house: talk to Amanda, talk to the boy, look around the home he grew up in.]**

---

## 7. The desert below the hills: night

*Moonlight. Young Spock walks out alone with a small pack. Behind him, slow and determined, comes I-Chaya.*

**YOUNG SPOCK:** Go back. This is mine to do. *(The sehlat sits down and simply looks at him.)* ...Fine. But keep up.

*Further back, a hooded figure follows at a distance: Selek.*

*A low snarl from the rocks. A LE-MATYA, a lean, striped desert hunter, drops into their path. I-Chaya charges it. They tangle and roar, and the sehlat is raked across the shoulder. Selek reaches the beast, takes hold of its neck, and it collapses.*

**SPOCK:** *(to the boy)* Are you hurt?

**YOUNG SPOCK:** No. *(staring at the unconscious le-matya)* How did you do that?

**SPOCK:** Practice. A great deal of it.

*I-Chaya tries to stand and can't. The wound is small, but the edges are darkening.*

**SPOCK:** Its claws carry poison. He needs a healer, tonight.

**YOUNG SPOCK:** He's too heavy to carry. *(He thinks, and then he decides.)* I'll bring the healer here. I know the way.

**SPOCK:** The desert is dangerous at night.

**YOUNG SPOCK:** Then I will be careful. Stay with him. Please.

*The boy runs. Spock kneels beside the old sehlat and rests a hand on its head.*

**SPOCK:** *(quietly)* Rest. The boy is faster than he thinks he is.

**[PLAY LATER: the boy's crossing back to the city, with hazards to get past.]**

---

## 8. Shi'Kahr: the healer's door, before dawn

*Young Spock hammers on a carved door. An elderly HEALER opens it.*

**HEALER:** It is very early to be knocking, child.

**YOUNG SPOCK:** My sehlat was clawed by a le-matya. He's in the foothills. Please come now.

**HEALER:** *(taking in the dust, the scrapes, the steadiness)* You crossed the desert alone, at night, to fetch me?

**YOUNG SPOCK:** Yes.

**HEALER:** Then I will not waste your effort by asking more questions. Fetch my bag.

---

## 9. The desert below the hills: dawn

*The healer kneels by I-Chaya. Selek and young Spock stand together.*

**HEALER:** The poison is in his blood now, Spock. I cannot draw it out. What I can do is end his pain. That decision belongs to the one he followed into the desert.

*The boy looks at Selek. Selek does not answer for him.*

**YOUNG SPOCK:** *(a long breath)* Then end it. He shouldn't hurt because of me.

*The healer works. Young Spock sits with the sehlat's great head in his lap as the sun comes up.*

**YOUNG SPOCK:** *(not looking up)* Is it wrong that I want to cry?

**SPOCK:** No. Wanting to is not wrong. Choosing what you do with it is what makes you who you are.

**[PLAY LATER: this whole scene as a playable choice, with every way of saving I-Chaya tried and failing.]**

---

## 10. Sarek's house: morning

*Sarek and Amanda in the doorway as young Spock and Selek return. Amanda goes to her son. Sarek waits.*

**YOUNG SPOCK:** Father. I-Chaya is dead. I chose it. It was the right thing for him.

**SAREK:** *(after a moment)* Then you chose well. *(and then, quieter)* He was a good companion to us both.

*Young Spock nods and goes inside with his mother. Sarek turns to Selek.*

**SAREK:** You followed him into the desert. Why?

**SPOCK:** Because someone once followed me.

**SAREK:** *(he does not quite understand, and lets it go)* My door will be open to you, cousin.

**SPOCK:** I will not pass this way again. But let him make his mistakes in front of you, Sarek. He will learn more from them than from any test.

*Selek raises his hand in the Vulcan salute, and Sarek returns it. Selek walks away into the morning light and does not look back.*

---

## 11. The Guardian's planet: night

*The ring flares. Spock steps out. Kirk is waiting, alone, sitting on the same fallen block McCoy sat on.*

**KIRK:** Well?

**SPOCK:** History is as it was. Almost. The sehlat died.

**KIRK:** I'm sorry, Spock.

**SPOCK:** *(looking back at the ring)* So was I, at seven. I had simply forgotten.

*Kirk gets up and puts a hand on Spock's shoulder for a second.*

**KIRK:** Kirk to Enterprise. Two to beam up.

---

## 12. Enterprise: transporter room

*Kirk and Spock shimmer in. SCOTTY is at the console; DR McCOY waits on the deck.*

**SCOTTY:** Two up, Captain. And both of them the ones I sent.

**McCOY:** Spock, you're overdue for your physical. And for the record, I knew who you were the whole time.

**SPOCK:** Of course you did, Doctor.

*Kirk steps down. Spock stays on the pad a moment longer, thinking of a fat, shaggy old sehlat.*

**END**
